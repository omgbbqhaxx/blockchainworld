3D Printed re-usable ABS molds.

When concrete is poured into molds, the most critical factor is preserving the exact geometry every time. However, during demolding, the mold must also be flexible enough to deform slightly so the cast can be removed without damage — and then return precisely to its original shape. This balance between elasticity and dimensional stability is essential. Fully ABS-based, specially tuned mold walls provide the flexibility needed for easy release, while the rigid PLA base layer ensures that the structure always snaps back to its exact original position after each use.
